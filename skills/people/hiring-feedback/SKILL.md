# Hiring Feedback

Generates signal-based interview evaluations with hire recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Collects interview session notes
2. Identifies key signals and competencies
3. Evaluates against role requirements
4. Generates structured feedback
5. Outputs hire recommendation with reasoning

## Usage

Provide candidate name, role applied for, and interview details. Include all feedback from interviewers.
